# 10. Questions for an Asheville outreach partner

For a first conversation with a prospective host: a day center, street-outreach team or low-barrier
shelter. About 60 minutes. Ask for examples without names. Nothing here assumes the organization
will participate.

**How you notice absences today**

1. When someone you usually see stops coming, how do you find out? Who notices, and how long does
   it usually take?
2. What happens next? Who decides to look, what do you try, how long do you keep trying, and where
   is it written down? What makes you stop?
3. What do you already record about visits or contact (sign-in sheets, HMIS, the by-name list,
   memory)? What would Heartbeat duplicate, and what would it add?
4. Without names: tell us about a time someone fell through the cracks. What would have caught it?

**Continuity**

5. How do you coordinate with other organizations about a particular person, and with what consent?
6. During and after Hurricane Helene, how did you keep — or lose — track of the people you serve?
   What would have helped most?
7. When a staff member leaves or is away, how does what they know about people get handed over?

**Capacity**

8. Who could realistically act as responders and as coordinator, for how many hours a week, on which
   days and hours? When does your outreach workload spike?

**The people you serve**

9. Who do you think would want this, and who would not? What would make it feel like surveillance
   or like a condition of getting help?
10. How often do people you serve have a working phone, and how often do numbers change? What
    languages, literacy and accessibility needs should the consent conversation and cards meet?

**Policies and obligations**

11. What are your current policies on requests from police or immigration authorities, on
    emergencies, on confidentiality, and on mandatory reporting? Are you a HIPAA covered entity, a
    victim service provider, or a federally assisted substance-use program, and do you have HMIS
    obligations through Continuum of Care funding?
12. Who on your side would review consent wording and a data agreement?

**Fit**

13. What would make this not worth your time? What would you need to see after eight weeks to keep
    going?
